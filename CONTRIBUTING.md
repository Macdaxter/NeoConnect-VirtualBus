# Contributing and Beta Reports

Public beta reports should describe one reproducible configuration. Do not
combine binaries from different versions.

Include:

- VirtualBus release version.
- Launcher, MAME, MiSTer HPS/core and ESP build identities.
- Game/profile, BIOS, region, DIP/Soft-DIP and clock profile.
- Cabinet roles and IDs.
- Active transport and topology.
- Exact steps, expected result and actual result.
- Relevant local logs with secrets, public IPs and credentials removed.

Do not attach ROMs, BIOS files, CHDs, copyrighted game data, passwords or SSH
keys. A hash mismatch or refused configuration is evidence; do not edit logs to
make the session appear successful.

New profiles and integrations should target the published protocol behavior and
identify themselves as third-party or experimental unless accepted into the
official reference.
